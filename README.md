# Create: Back on Track

> **Meta Description**  
> Discover Create: Back on Track – an innovative Minecraft modpack featuring 180+ mods, 15 chapters, and 15 exclusive machines. Build advanced contraptions and automated factories for an unparalleled Create experience!

## 📌 Overview

Create: Back on Track builds upon the acclaimed *Create: Above & Beyond*, pushing automation and engineering to new heights.  
With over **180 integrated mods**, this modpack delivers a structured experience across **15 chapters**, featuring more than **80 contraptions** and **15 exclusive Create-based machines**.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **15 Progressive Chapters**  
  Unlock new mechanics and challenges through structured progression.

- **80+ Contraptions and Exclusive Machines**  
  Build everything from alloy factories to rocket engine production lines, including munitions plants, oil processors, and semiconductor facilities.

- **Full Automation Experience**  
  Combine tech, mechanical, and magic mods to create fully automated industrial complexes.

- **Custom Resource Packs**  
  - *BetterVanillaBuilding* by StefanJ2_  
  - *Create Style Redstone Pen* by Wedge1453

- **Frequent Updates & Support**  
  Developed and maintained by **spydnel** to ensure continuous improvements and compatibility.

## 💻 System Requirements & Installation

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1  
- **Mod Loader:** Forge  
- **Recommended Specs:** A powerful PC with sufficient RAM and CPU to handle large mod integrations.  
- **Installation:** Install via CurseForge Launcher or alternatives like MultiMC/Prism.

## 🎯 Why Choose Create: Back on Track?

- Push your **creativity and automation** to new levels.
- Face **unique progression challenges**.
- Join an **active community** on Discord and CurseForge.

## 💡 Tips for Best Experience

1. Follow chapters and quests to unlock new tech.
2. Use tutorials and guides for optimal builds.
